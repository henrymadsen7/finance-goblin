# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted contact. Respect people's privacy, especially when discussing financial data. Do not post another person's account details, credentials, transactions, or identifying information.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Raise concerns privately through an available maintainer contact or GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Do not publish private details as part of a conduct report. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
